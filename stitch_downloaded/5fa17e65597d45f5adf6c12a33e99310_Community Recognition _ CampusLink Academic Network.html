Calibration Methodology

CampusLink metrics are designed to discourage superficial forum volume. Score weighting is governed strictly through verifiable scholarly utility:

  1. 1
    Peer-Reviewed Technical Solutions Solutions accepted by verified researchers carry a 3.5× weight compared to unverified upvotes.
  2. 2
    Open Science Artifacts Reproducible dataset uploads, validated schematics, and preprints archived with permanent DOIs.
  3. 3
    Cross-Institution Mentorship Synchronous debugging sessions and sprint guidance between scholars across different state colleges.
